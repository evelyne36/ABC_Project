## 1. 设计（正式规划口径，勿改）

- 路由集合：**55 条** = 合成 Regime / Sparse / Curved 的 45 条 Full 路由 + 真实数据 10 条（复用 R0 与 N4 模型）。
- 另需 **42 个 CGB** 补足合成路由（这是 N5 唯一的额外拟合成本）。
- 对同一路由 **stagewise refit k=1/2/3**，共 **165** 次系数模型拟合；`raw-k1` 作控制（区分 bin 代表值 vs raw 特征的差异）；**affine k=1** 为可选第 55 组。
- 纪律：var 在训练内选，正则冻结；**不得**在已含 inner-val 标签的 outer 路由上调参；结论只能写成"**固定路由容量 probe**"，**不能**称为"完整 k>1 的 CrossGB"（因为 split search 本身没改）。

## 3. English appendix draft（骨架）

**Table S6.** Capacity probe on fixed routing: for 55 leaf-routing structures (45 synthetic + 10 real), the predictive leaf is refitted with k = 1, 2, 3 features per leaf under identical splits, shrinkage and regularization. Also reported: a raw-feature k=1 control (isolating the bin-fit / raw-apply gap) and an affine k=1 variant.

*Notes.* Routing is held fixed, so this measures the **capacity of the leaf model at a given partition**, not the performance of a full CrossGB variant with k>1 integrated into split search; the latter is left to future work.
