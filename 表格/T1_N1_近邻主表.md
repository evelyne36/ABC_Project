**中性事实句（可写进材料，不含解释）**：
- 中：在五个真实数据集的固定协议下，CrossGB 均未取得 boosted 家族的最低 test RMSE；相对 XGBoost，CrossGB 在 4/5 数据集上 RMSE 更低，在 cpu_activity 上高 79.92%。
- En: Under a shared protocol across five real datasets, CrossGB never attains the lowest test RMSE within the boosted family; it improves on XGBoost on 4 of 5 datasets and is 79.92% worse on cpu_activity.

## 3. English appendix draft

**Table S1.** Nested-CV test RMSE (mean over 5 outer folds; lower is better) under a shared protocol: identical outer/inner splits and a shared 300-candidate pool per dataset. Best value per dataset is **bold** within the boosted family.

| Dataset | XGBoost | CrossGB | PL Trees | LinXGBoost | PILOT† | TAO† |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| cpu_activity | **2.1868** | 3.9344 | 17.7051 ‡ | 3.7781 § / 2.6677 ¶ | 2.4500 | 2.9049 |
| kin8nm | 0.1125 | 0.1003 | **0.0961** | 0.1030 | 0.1735 | 0.1236 |
| curved_control | 0.1631 | 0.1630 | **0.1535** | 0.1592 | 0.1558 | 0.3136 |
| feature_switch | 0.2304 | 0.2158 | 0.2142 | **0.1528** | 2.2633 | 0.9419 |
| regime_slope | 0.3399 | 0.2375 | 0.2951 | **0.1526** | 3.4412 | 0.8762 |

*Notes.* All methods use the same nested splits and the same 300-candidate configuration pool, with model selection performed on inner validation folds only; numbers are outer-fold test RMSE.
† PILOT and TAO are used in their official **single-tree** form, so their errors are not directly comparable to the boosted methods; they are reported for completeness.
‡ GBDT-PL on cpu_activity: one of five outer folds diverges (79.68); the median over folds is 2.1917. The mean is reported together with the median rather than dropping the fold.
§ LinXGBoost under a strict 600 s per-candidate budget, frozen to a 20/300 candidate subsample (every 15th candidate): 12 of 20 candidates time out on every cpu_activity fold, so this number is censored and must not be read as an equal-budget result. On the other four datasets 2–3 of 20 candidates time out.
¶ LinXGBoost cpu_activity under the relaxed 2400 s budget, 4 of 5 folds (outer 5 missing).
