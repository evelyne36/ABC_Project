## 1. 设计（已冻结）

- 预算：每个候选模型 **60 s / 300 s / 900 s** 的 wall-clock 上限（单线程、固定线程数）。
- 面板：`cpu_activity`（CrossGB 输）、`kin8nm`（CrossGB 赢）、`energy_efficiency`（近平）。
- 对每个预算，XGBoost 在该预算内**扩树/调参**（不是固定树数），CrossGB 同样受限；两者都在 inner 折上选优，只在 outer 折上报 test。
- 结论只能写成"**相同搜索时间上限下**的性能"，不能写成"相同 FLOPs"或"相同总成本"。

## 3. English appendix draft（骨架）

**Table S3.** Compute-matched comparison: both methods are constrained to the same wall-clock search budget per candidate (60/300/900 s), with XGBoost free to grow trees and re-tune within that budget. Values are outer-fold test RMSE after inner-fold model selection.

*Notes.* Budgets are wall-clock seconds on a single fixed thread count on the same host, not FLOPs and not total end-to-end training time; a run that exhausts the budget mid-training is reported together with the fraction of candidates truncated.
